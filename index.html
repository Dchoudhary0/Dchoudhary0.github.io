About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Arizona with a minor in Business Administration. What drives me is the place where technology, creativity, and people meet — building things that are technically solid but also genuinely enjoyable to use.

I work hands-on with AI tools at the University's AI+ Studio, helping students and staff explore generative AI and use it responsibly. I love turning ideas into real, usable things — whether that's a browser game, a promotional design, or helping someone feel less intimidated by a new tool. When I'm not coding, you'll find me teaching, leading teams, or planting trees.

I speak English, Hindi, Punjabi, and a little French — and I believe good communication is the most underrated tech skill.

Dream Collision

A browser-based escape game with 4 levels, 3 worlds, and 8 playable characters, deployed live on Render.

My contribution: I led the creative vision — designing the game concept, four levels, three worlds, and eight characters. I used AI prompt engineering to generate and refine all the artwork and visual themes through iterative prompting, then built the front-end UI and interactions in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I debugged gameplay logic and layout issues, and handled deployment and cross-browser testing on Render.
